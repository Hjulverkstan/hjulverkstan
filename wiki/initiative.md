# The initiative

Hjulverkstan is an initiative Save the Children Sweden started in Gothenburg, to support integration and empower the people who live nearby. Its means are bike workshops: the bikes are the medium, and the heart is the community that grows around a workshop and what it does for the people in it.

## 1. What a workshop offers

A workshop offers three services, all free: it repairs bikes, rents them out, and teaches people to cycle. It also employs people, so a workshop is a place to work as well as to visit.

## 2. Where Alten comes in

As the network of workshops grows, so does the need for a system that keeps track of bikes, customers, employees and services, and for a public website that tells more people about the work. Alten Sweden builds both, as one application, pro bono, through its interns and its consultants between assignments, and leads the technical development.

The work began early in 2024. How it went, period by period, and what each period built is told in [the repository's README](../README.md) and not yet brought in here. When the README was written, the application was not yet released to production; how the workshops use it today is not yet said here.

## 3. Joining

The initiative welcomes contributions from anyone, person or organisation, who shares its vision of stronger, more inclusive communities. The software is open source, and developers, designers and writers can all take part; how, by a pull request, in GitHub Discussions or by getting in touch, is in [the repository's README](../README.md).
