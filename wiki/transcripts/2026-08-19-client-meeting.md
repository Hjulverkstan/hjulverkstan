## What this phase is · 00:03, 11:55

Monday and Tuesday went to how this phase of the work with the client starts. Mauricio and the interns have no history with the project, and that is as it should be — they are fresh, space is given for it, nothing is rushed today. The aim is still a proper plan and everything needed for it; if that is not reached today, the next meeting follows as soon as possible.

What the project has lacked all along is a knowledge structure, and that is what the Alten team is building. What a knowledge structure is is still being explored, but it contains everything about the product, the client and the background for it — all the understanding, from the highest goals and values down to the detailed requirements of the variants [?] of the system: all the knowledge needed to generate a user manual, or to take a feature request — anyone's idea — and see how it would affect the system, the previous decisions, the values of the system. That is the crucial part that never existed. Into it go also conventions — how we work, how we think — and other aspects relevant to Alten as the vendor [?] rather than to the client. It is why the meeting is recorded: whatever useful is raised is extracted and put in the structure in its right place. The structure is for humans and for AI alike — an AI outperforms once it is bounded in an environment holding all the knowledge it needs: product knowledge, background, technical knowledge and principles, access to the code, to the project plan and the priorities. And it grounds people: four of the team are new, and what happened before was verbal talk until a member was fit to do something — talk that never stops; the structure is the basis for getting someone going and contributing, and that involves going over the product and how it works. We are also colleagues going through learnings together, students of various aspects of life in this work, so some of the personal is recorded too — how we feel, what it is to be new at something — because these are crucial aspects of learning; Jona's view is that whoever is taken onto a team, it is the person who is hired, and where they are as people, what they are navigating, is exactly what is met in work life. That is one example of the facets folded in.

Jona has a good feeling about the phase. There are no grand plans in the right area [?]; the knowledge is being collected properly, with the focus on how things are kept together and the source they emanate from. Before, the project ran ahead a lot, very technically — a lot was put in place, but that is not how things grow organically. The structure being built, knowledge-wise and process-wise, is meant to hold a space for the project as it progresses, and to keep Alten and the client in touch in a good way; there are many facets to feel and talk about when they come. The project is not set in Alten either, and the foundation helps there too: with everything connected, values can be extrapolated clearly and clear directives given as to why things are done.

## What a Hjulverkstan is · 52:50, 1:01:17

A basic Hjulverkstan has everything included, because it is not just a bike shop. It is a place for youths to grow and come back into society; a recycling place; a place to be involved in the community and do positive things in it, open to people to gather, come and go. Its placement is tactical: some of the workshops stand in front of big problem areas, because the intent is that the area becomes more open for everyone, not only for those who sell drugs. They are on the torg, in community places — not hidden; the only hidden one was Hjällbo, and that was fixed with a container in the middle of the place, so you never miss it ("you have to be like forty people" [?] — and they appreciate it). No place has ever been robbed or damaged, because each is a place for the people: it is not Save the Children that opened a bike shop, it is the people's bike shop in Backa, in Frölunda. That is the important thing.

The shops are not for everyone: they are for those who cannot afford a bike or to fix their own, which is why they are tactically placed in socio-economically difficult places; putting one up next to someone earning their bread on repairing bikes — Jona's words, Chiaco's agreement — is a no-no [?]. Hence there are not many more places to open in Gothenburg: the need is not the same everywhere. Östra [?] needs a shop and has asked before, but the answer was not yet — they were not ready, many of the organisers wanted to do something else — because the aim is to come to a new place when they need us and to be involved with the rest, not to arrive as an external part.

But we are not done, Chiaco says, we can do so much more, and this help is something big — with the website and the portal it is for real, next level; that was nothing Chiaco had in mind, it was so far away, because it is expensive — "you are expensive, but I love our —".

## The portal: tickets, process and product · 28:50

The heart of the portal is tickets. A ticket connects inventory, employee and customer. A ticket is of a type — a repair or a rental, sometimes the receiving or the giving away of a bike — and there is nuance beyond that: a rental can be of various sorts and dynamics, for an organisation or a person; a repair can be of a customer's bike or of an internal bike yet to be processed. The tickets are the data — the process data — for a process the client wants established anyway: from the posters and discussions seen out at Hjulverkstan, there is a clear picture of what is done when a bike comes in, what a service is, and so on; there is a timeline and there are non-technical instructions, and at some points the portal is used. It is one process, and the product is already part of it. So the two topics — using the application and repairing bikes — should not be conflated, but they are highly interconnected and cannot be separated. How the knowledge is shipped — a database, in the portal or on the website — is the end result, not the focus; the focus is the goals, the requirements, and what they can enable. This needs to be honoured properly; it is a crucial part of the next phase of priority — the client embodying and using the portal — and will take reflection and prioritising.
