# How this wiki is made

This wiki is written under the rule, a way of laying knowledge so that the biggest understanding comes first and every part can be read whole. It is made from recorded meetings and from what the code and its documents show.

What the wiki is for comes first here: whom it serves, and so why it is written as it is.

[What this wiki is for](vision.md)

Anyone who writes here reads the rule once before they start. It is kept in a repository of its own, which this one includes, and only the rule itself is placed here; its study, its tools and the thinking of the person who wrote it are reached through [its entry](the-rule/README.md).

[The rule](the-rule/rule.md)

The rule says how knowledge is laid, but building a part of this wiki with an agent also takes people, at a few points the rule leaves to them. What a part is, and what helped us as people while building one, comes next, for whoever builds the next part.

[How the team builds a part of the wiki](building.md)

Someone new will also need to know how to start working with an agent at all, and [a beginner's guide]() for the interns who come next is owed for that.

Much of what the wiki is to hold about Hjulverkstan is meant to come from meetings, with Save the Children and within the team. Each meeting is written up as knowledge, and everyone in it approves it before anything is built from it.

[Transcripts](transcripts/README.md)

Working this way with an agent has given the team reflections of its own, the reasons behind much of how we build: what the agent keeps and what it loses, and where people are needed. They are reflections rather than facts, and still being added to.

[The team's reflections](reflections.md)

How the wiki came to be what it is, change by change, is kept in [the log](log/README.md).
