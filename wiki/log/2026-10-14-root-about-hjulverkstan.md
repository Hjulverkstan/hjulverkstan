# 2026-10-14 — The root is about Hjulverkstan

`wiki/README.md` was rewritten from a page about the wiki into a root about Hjulverkstan, and two parts were written for it: `initiative.md` and `making.md`. The structure was talked through over two days with an agent, outline first, and the team settled it before anything was written.

## What was decided

- **The root is about Hjulverkstan**, not about the wiki or the rule, since the wiki lives in Hjulverkstan's public repository and is to grow into a medium for learning about it and joining it. *In force, the team's decision.*

- **The root has four parts, in this order:** the initiative; a guide to the portal, owed; the pipeline; how this wiki is made. Headings were dropped from the root, so each part is a sentence and a card, and a part gets its card only once it holds a level. *In force, the team's decision.*

- **The background is the root's own face**, with the initiative in depth beneath it, rather than a separate holon. The rule's own background stays in the rule, placed and not retold.

- **The portal guide** will stand where *what Alten builds* was outlined. It is owed, marked by an empty link.

- **"How it is built" was cut** as its own part. The more technical knowledge is to grow around the pipeline. *Lean:* when it does, a holon above the pipeline places it as one part, so the pipeline's face stays true.

- **How this wiki is made** places the rule (`rule.md` alone, not the rule's whole entry) and the transcripts, links the log, and owes three parts: why Hjulverkstan took up the rule, how the team builds a part of the wiki, and the team's reflections.

- **The initiative is drawn only from the repository's README**, since the transcripts are not yet approved. The README's background stays where it is until the team decides whether the README shrinks to point here.

- The root's old note on `pipeline-next/` replacing the earlier pipeline was dropped. It stands in [the entry of 2026-10-06](2026-10-06-basics-into-pipeline-next.md).
