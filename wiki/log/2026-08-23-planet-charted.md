# 2026-08-23 — The planet charted; the outward study's first pass

Agent session at Jona's instruction: a fleet of parallel agents, one per territory, five minutes wall-clock, tasked to research the outward study and chart the ground the wiki's questions already stand on. Eleven territories, each briefed with the fifteen-rung ladder and told to hunt contradictions rather than confirmations.

**Created.** [`drafts/chart.md`](../drafts/chart.md) — the survey. Nine seeded areas from `study.md` plus two of its standing questions taken as territories of their own (*bounded heads*, *compounding*). Each fold carries what the practice holds, what it says to the ladder, what to steal, what it costs, its sources, and what it leaves open. The file leads with the cross-cutting findings, because the convergence between territories is worth more than any single one.

**What the pass found.** *One fact, one home* is the ladder's most-contradicted rung — Diátaxis, Wikipedia's summary style, the slip-box and Bruner's spiral all push against it independently, while cognitive load theory's redundancy effect defends it harder than we do. Two reconciliations came back: redundancy across levels is spiral and within a level is drift; and one home is a rule about authorship, not reachability. *Written whole* collides with measurement — models extract better from shuffled text than from logically ordered text, and Nygard is categorical that a decision record must never be rewritten. Rung 1's equation between comprehension and context window holds in direction and breaks in quantity: about four chunks against a token budget that cannot chunk, an agent that re-reads for free against a person who cannot, and what they actually share is retrieval cost. Nesting has numbers behind it — two levels, three at most. Every practice that scaled has a closed status vocabulary *and* somebody working the queue. And the boundary against code came back cleanest of all: prose owns why and forces, code owns what and how, and prose restating code is a defect, not redundancy.

**The gap it names.** The ladder has no rung for enforcement. Our bar — an agent could rebuild the front end from the wiki alone — is a test nobody runs, and every territory that scaled had a check or a queue behind its markers. The chart proposes running the bar as an exercise rather than holding it as an aspiration.

**One correction to carry.** Rung 14's likely citations are mostly folklore. The 1:10:100 defect-cost ratio and the "IBM Systems Sciences Institute" study behind it trace to no published research; Boehm's curve replicates in direction but not in shape; NIST's $59.5B and the CHAOS figures are rhetoric. The solid ground is absorptive capacity and knowledge as a non-rival good, and the current wording — *understanding before acting is cheap; remedy after is not* — reads as a 1:10:100 echo and should be reworded toward Cunningham's own framing of debt as deferred consolidation.

**Eight proposals to the ladder** are listed at the end of the chart. None is ruled; they are what the evidence asks, put where it can be argued with.

**Rewritten.** [`drafts/study.md`](../drafts/study.md) whole — the standing questions now say what the pass moved and what it left, the areas list holds only what the fleet did *not* reach (design and Figma, localisation, derived documentation, teaching in a non-developer register, who pays, the vehicle), and the inward half is stated as not started, with the one demand the outward pass already makes of it.

**Held open.** The chart's ground was gathered in one pass and no human has followed the links; every citation is a lead until read. Two are flagged as not-to-cite. The fleet was briefed by us and carried our framing into its own findings, so a second, differently-briefed pass is the honest check and has not been run. `knowledge-structure.md` is untouched — the proposals are addressed to the team, not applied.
