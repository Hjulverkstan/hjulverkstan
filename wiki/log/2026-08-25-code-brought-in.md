Every rewritten link was checked and resolves.

**What it proposes.** The fifteen rungs compressed into **two values** — understanding reaches the head that acts; nothing is claimed beyond what is held — **eight facts** about the world, and **six principles**, each carrying the trace of which value met which fact (`V1×6,8`), so a principle is disputed by denying its value or denying its fact and there is no third way in. Six familiar virtues are demoted to derivations rather than values of their own, so the two-value set stays auditable. Beyond the principles it carries what our draft does not: a mechanism layer (the *field*, registers, readings, a *frontier* index of open questions with an owner), an entry rule, a ten-step method, and a test suite in which every test names its runner and its moment.

**Three of the chart's corrections are folded in**, which is worth recording since we raised them: the person-and-model bound is *not* an equation — bounded differently, and what they share is retrieval cost; the cost-of-late-understanding claim is kept only in its grounded form, with the 1:10:100 ratio and the "IBM Systems Sciences Institute" study named as folklore not to be cited; and status vocabularies survive only closed, dated and owned.

**Status, and what is ours to rule.** Proposed in its own project — research, not law, awaiting its author's ratification there — and binding on nothing here, since `wiki/drafts/` never is. It is written to *supersede* our principles; nothing obliges us to take it whole. `knowledge-structure.md` is therefore untouched apart from a board line pointing at it. What the code knowingly leaves out matters more here than there and is listed in the study: the rung on personal knowledge and its speaker's ownership — the transcripts practice rests on it — and everything client-side, which is the delivery goal. Its own *Open* list, ordered by bite, is the agenda for the team hour.
