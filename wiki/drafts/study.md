## Where the study stands

Three files sit beside this one, and they are meant to be read in this order.

[`chart.md`](chart.md) is the outward pass — eleven territories tasted in parallel, each with what the practice holds, what it says to our ladder, what to steal and what it costs. Its ground was gathered in one pass and **no human has followed the links**, so every citation is a lead; two claims are flagged as things not to cite.

[`code.md`](code.md) is the sharpest input we have, and it did not come from here. It is written in **OpenLight**, Jona's own project, which is chasing the same question from the other side; our ladder and chart were among its inputs. It has just been cut to a fifth of its length and is much better for it. What it now states is only the core: **one limit** — nobody holds everything, and a person forgetting and a context window filling are the same wall in two currencies — **two primitives**, the *fold* (a piece of writing that stands on its own) and the *link* (the one join, and always an offer, never a missing piece), and **two laws**, one for each side of compounding:

- **Nothing arrives before its ground.** Every meaning is given before it is used, purpose comes before machinery, every stopping point leaves the reader whole — and where the subject genuinely forces the other order, the debt is declared in a line. Borrowing is allowed; silent borrowing is not.
- **The ground stays true.** Every fact keeps one home and every claim carries its confidence — with *settled* meaning settled for now, given what it stands on, since a new piece can arrive and change what the old ones meant.

Everything the earlier version carried above that core — the registers, the readings, the method's ten steps, the named tests — is gone from the document. That is a deliberate narrowing, not a loss of nerve: the code now says plainly that **nothing has been built under it yet**, and that how folds compose into a whole is to be *observed, not prescribed*. Its closing link points at a first proof that does not exist.

**That gap is the opening for us.** The code needs practical experience to grow, and this repository is a real structure with real readers — interns, a client, and agents — which is exactly the ground a proof needs. The next step, already agreed: use the code to generate a structure for the sweep pass OpenLight has run, and let the poc side of the code grow from what that teaches. Research is the priority right now; the proof is how the research earns its keep.

The inward half has not started.

## First peer review

Eric read the draft on 2026-08-28 — the first review of this work by someone outside its writing — and his notes and the rewrite converged without either seeing the other. He asked for *fold* to be explained early rather than assumed; the new draft explains it at length. He named *chunk*, *field* and *substrate* as words used before they were earned; all three are gone. He asked that the text around a link say what is on the other side before you click, so the reader can choose; the code now states that as law. What he asked for and the code did not do: visuals and diagrams as a tool for human readers, and restatement in a plainer register at the densest points. Both are open, and both are his to press.

The lesson worth keeping: the review's value was not in catching errors but in showing where the writing had run ahead of its reader — which is law 1, found from the outside by someone who had not read the law.

## Questions we stand with

Where the chart moved one it says so; where the code answers one it says so — and an answered question is answered as a proposal.

- **Bounded heads.** *Charted: roughly four chunks, not seven; chunking is stored structure, not capacity. The code makes this its one limit and drops the equation — better tools widen what is held and cheapen the reaching, but they do not change how understanding works.*
- **Nesting.** *Charted: two levels, three at most, breadth over depth, labels judged by whether a reader picks the right child from the label alone. The code now declines to rule on depth at all and files it under what must be observed.*
- **Order.** Is a reading order one line or a tree? *Open again, on purpose. The earlier version answered it with published readings; the current one does not, and asks for practice first.*
- **Outcomes and audiences.** *Still the inward study's first question, and still ours alone — the code does not reach it.*
- **The boundary against code.** *Charted and ruled into a line: prose owns why and forces, code owns what and how, and prose restating what the code says is a defect, not thoroughness.*
- **Ground and status.** *Charted: closed vocabularies, dated, owned. The code keeps the substance — confidence is part of the writing, an unmarked guess becomes law by default — and drops the vocabulary, which is ours to choose.*
- **Compounding.** *Charted, and the code carries the correction: the 1:10:100 ratio and the "IBM Systems Sciences Institute" study behind it are folklore and must not be cited.*
- **The vehicle.** *The code now answers this the plain way: no new technology is needed. Files in folders under version control hold the structure, and existing agent harnesses are its natural match — a session started against the structure becomes what it reads.*

## Areas to taste

The nine seeded areas have each had one pass in [`chart.md`](chart.md); a second, differently-briefed pass is the honest check and has not been run. What the chart did not reach:

- **Design systems and Figma** as a linkable source of design truth.
- **Localisation** — what a Swedish-reading client and Swedish-speaking mechanics do to an English wiki.
- **Derived documentation** — OpenAPI, Javadoc, JSDoc, generated tables: where "generate, don't retype" gets tested.
- **Teaching in a non-developer register** — task cards, demonstration, how the mechanics actually learn the portal and the trade.
- **Who pays for documentation** in a pro-bono project staffed by interns and consultants between assignments.
- **Visuals** — diagrams as a carrier of understanding, and whether what helps a person costs an agent anything. Eric's ask; unexplored.

## What is ours to decide

- **Whether the code replaces the ladder, sits beside it, or is taken in parts.** It is written to state a core, not to cover our ground; the fifteen rungs still hold things it does not reach.
- **What it does not fold in, that matters here:** the personal is knowledge and its speaker owns it — the transcripts practice rests on it — and everything client-side, which is the delivery goal.
- **The human reader is not blank.** Onboarding research says mentors and response latency dominate, not documentation. Concrete for us: our next readers are interns and a client.
- **Our register vocabulary** — drafts, log, transcripts, and a *spec* that is still an open word.
